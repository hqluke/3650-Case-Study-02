1. WHAT I DECIDED

- The flow: one combined booking flow for Davis Park and Valley View, the two
  Davis County public courses from my Phase 1 audit. The findings drove the
  design, and the mapping from finding to design is in README section 1. The
  short version: one Holes control instead of filter buttons that silently
  decide what loads (H-01), an empty state that says which of "sold out," "not
  open yet," or "wrong class" it is and offers searches with times (H-02), both
  courses in one list with a count and earliest time each (H-03), closed days
  shown as disabled with the date they open (H-04), cancellation deadlines on
  the pay step (H-05), and the day and time on the Pay button (H-06, A-01,
  A-05).
- Hosting: GitHub Pages rather than Figma.
- Build order: I asked for the whole thing to be built end to end first and
  committed after.
- Iterations: two rounds on the design. I asked that only the final build and
  spec files be included in this repository.
- I reviewed the local build and the spec files myself and directed the fixes
  in section 4.

4. TESTING AND FIXES

Claude ran keyboard-only passes, axe-core, Lighthouse, the accessibility tree
in Chrome, and both audit scripts, and reviewed screenshots at 390 px and
1280 px. The problems it found and fixed:

- Error summary links were 32 px tall with no gap. Fixed: 44 px minimum height
  and 8 px between links.
- Arrow keys in the date picker dropped focus to the page body after one press,
  because every change rebuilt the date chips. Fixed: the chips are updated in
  place.
- The checkmark on selected chips and the "!" on error messages were read as
  part of the control's accessible name. Fixed: CSS content alt text set to
  empty.
- The Pay button never showed "Processing payment..." while loading, because
  the total was recalculated after the label was set. Fixed the order.
- The results summary showed the previous day while the next day was loading.
  Fixed: it says "Loading tee times for <day>..." while the skeleton is up.
- The middle cancellation deadline was printed backwards. Fixed the order.
- If two filters blocked everything at once, the empty state had no
  suggestion. Added one that loosens time of day and holes together and keeps
  the group size.
- On a phone the step list and date chips wrapped badly and the Filters arrow
  dropped to the corner. Fixed the layout.

After the fixes: 0 axe violations across 7 page states, Lighthouse
accessibility 100, every target at least 44 x 44 px with 8 px spacing, every
color pair passes WCAG 2.1 AA. The raw results are in
prototype_spec/contrast-results.md, prototype_spec/target-size-audit.md, and
prototype_spec/axe-results.md.

These fixes were made by Claude under my direction. I reviewed them by
checking the page and the audit reports, and I approved them.
